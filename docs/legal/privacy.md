# Privacy

- Collect the minimum: clip metadata, pose keypoints, derived KPIs, consent records.
- Store raw video in object storage with short-lived signed URLs. Do not git raw media.
- Athletes can export and delete passport data.
- Minors: parental consent before upload.
- FERPA: school-linked rosters are education records when the school is the customer.
- Retention default: raw video 90 days unless pinned to a passport version.
