# Product

## Thesis

Turn a phone video of a 10/20/40, start, or position-specific burst into a closed loop:

`Capture → Assess → Prescribe → Train → Re-test → Verify → Value → Match`

Not a generic 40-time app. Movement DNA plus a comparable band.

## Who pays

HS/college skill players, sprint-conversion football athletes, position coaches, small collectives. Not the NFL in v1.

## MVP v1

- 2–3 clips (side + 45°)
- Markerless joints
- 6–8 cues max
- Templates: WR release, DB break, RB first cut, OL first step
- Before/after card + share clip + comparable band
- Optional NIL/recruiting range with uncertainty

## MVP v2

Guided capture quality score, WR + DB only, 6 cues with confidence, 3 drills/cue, re-test reminders, coach roster + annotate.
