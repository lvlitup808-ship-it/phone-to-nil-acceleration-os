# NIL valuation disclaimer

Acceleration OS NIL output is **informational**.

It is not a contract offer, a guarantee of deals, scholarship, draft capital, or playing time, legal/tax/agency advice, or an NCAA eligibility determination.

Every band payload includes a quantile range (p25–p75), confidence, explicit assumptions, and comp cluster IDs. Those numeric fields are **null** until a sourced comp dataset exists and the golden-set gate opens. Nothing is computed yet. No composite metric.

State and NCAA NIL rules change. The valuation service records `disclaimer_version` on every response.
