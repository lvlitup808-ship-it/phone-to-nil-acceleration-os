# Retake Packet

Send automatically when ingest rejects.

## Reason: fps_too_low

The clip was filmed at 24fps. We need 30fps minimum, 60fps preferred.

Fix: Open your camera settings → Video → select 1080p at 60fps. Re-film.

## Reason: too_short

The clip was 3 seconds. We need 5–10 seconds so the athlete completes 3 steps past the movement.

Fix: Start recording 1 second before the athlete moves, stop 3 steps after.

## Reason: phone_moved

The phone moved during the first second. We need a still frame to calibrate.

Fix: Set the phone down or brace your elbows. Hold still for 1 full second before the athlete moves.

## Reason: missing_angle

Only one angle uploaded. We need both side and 45°.

Fix: Film both. Side first, then move 45° and film again.

## Reason: no_field_lines

No yard lines visible. Calibration will fall back to height-only, which downgrades 2 of 6 cues.

Fix: Reframe so at least one yard line or hash mark is visible behind the athlete.
