# Labeling Session Rules

1. Label in sessions of 45 minutes max. Break for 15.
2. Do not label after 8pm. Fatigue shows in the numbers.
3. If you're unsure on a cue, mark it disputed. Do not guess.
4. Use the reference clip for each cue before labeling it. 3 seconds, watch it, then label.
5. Voice notes are fine. Type is fine. Choose one and stay consistent.
6. If a clip is bad (occluded, dark, wrong angle), mark cannot_label and move on. Do not force it.

What "good" looks like: your labels should match a second coach on 10 of 12 cues. If they don't, the cue definition is the problem, not you.
