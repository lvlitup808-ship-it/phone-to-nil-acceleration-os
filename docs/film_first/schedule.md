# 20-clip shooting schedule

Two sessions. One week apart.

## Session 1 — 3 WR + 3 DB

- 6 athletes, 4 clips each (side + 45 × 2 movements or 2 angles × 2 positions as assigned), 24 clips filmed
- Expect 6–8 to pass ingest after rejection
- Label within 48 hours

## Session 2 — 7 WR + 7 DB

- Remaining athletes
- Fill gaps from Session 1 rejections
- Label within 48 hours

## Week 3 — inter-rater + gate evaluation

- 2 coaches label 4 overlapping clips
- Compute ICC per cue
- RTMPose gate: pass or hold

If after Session 1 you have fewer than 4 clean clips, the problem is the capture protocol, not the athletes. Fix the protocol before Session 2.
