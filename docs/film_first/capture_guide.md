# Capture Guide

One page. Print it. Hand it to whoever films.

## Before you press record

- Phone at hip height, held steady, no handheld wobble
- 8 yards from the athlete, no zoom
- Side angle first, then 45°. Two clips per movement.
- Daylight or well-lit indoor. No backlight.
- Turf, grass, or track only. No gym floors.

## The shot

1. Athlete stands still for 1 full second. Camera stays still too.
2. Athlete performs the movement.
3. Let them run 3 steps past the finish.
4. Total clip: 5–10 seconds.

## Do not

- Do not zoom
- Do not walk with the phone
- Do not film from behind or from the front
- Do not film into the sun
- Do not clip the athlete's feet out of frame
- Do not film on a phone under 30fps

## Good vs bad

- Good side angle: full body, feet visible, yard lines in frame
- Bad side angle: chest up only, zoomed, moving camera
- Good 45°: full body, one yard line visible behind athlete
- Bad 45°: backlit, athlete small in frame, no field reference

If the clip doesn't look like the good examples, it will be rejected. Retake before uploading.
